# Peaceful Space — Digital Experience Framework

> Internal framework for keeping the storefront aligned with the brand's "unhurried conviction" voice. Read this before adding UI, especially anything that touches scarcity, urgency, or the buy moment. The brand is rooted in loss, resilience, "Trust Yourself," and a founder who builds everything in-house. Peace here means honest, transparent, weighted — not spa, not beige minimal, not corporate wellness.

## Philosophy — Unhurried Conviction

A quiet, organized workbench — not a spa, not a beige minimalist storefront, not luxury sluggishness. The site must respect the weight of the work: if every wallet is hand-cut from a single hide, the site cannot flash "3 viewing now!" in the customer's face. Peace here means honesty, transparency, and trusting the visitor to make an intentional choice without algorithmic coercion. Peaceful ≠ soft. Peaceful ≠ sanitized. The grief, the grind, "Gmoneyworld," the chrome accents, and "Trust Yourself" stay sharp. The manipulative ecommerce chrome does not.

## What's Already Aligned (lean in)

These existing patterns already signal intentionality. Don't rebuild — amplify:

- **The Archive (`pages/Archive.tsx`)** — sold-out items as historical record, not dead inventory. "Gone but not forgotten" is provenance, not stockout.
- **Founder Notes on the PDP** — customer-to-builder voice rendered in `pages/ProductDetails.tsx`, no marketing spin. Elevated to sit directly under the Add to bag button (gated on `!isUnavailable` so sold/archived pieces use the archival voice instead).
- **Numbered-Edition Pricing** — real scarcity in `services/numberedPieces.ts` ("12 / 44 minted at $75"). Authentic; doesn't need to yell. The tier callout in `pages/ProductDetails.tsx` is now neutral archival so the provenance stands on its own without yellow highlighting.
- **The Custom Inquiry Flow** — friction that proves workshop, not drop-shipper. Asks the customer to choose leather, thread, hardware, layout.
- **The Wallets landing page (`pages/Wallets.tsx`)** — provenance-first framing for 1/1 builds, no hype. Already aligned; no changes were needed during the wedge.
- **The `components/AnnouncementBar.tsx` brand line** — "Coalition · Hand-built in Baltimore · No factory, no shortcuts" sits above every page as a deliberate surface, not a sale.

## What Contradicts It (cut or reframe)

| Pattern | Why it fights the brand | File target |
|---|---|---|
| Pulsing red/green badges | Manufactured anxiety; reduces 1/1 art to a commodity race | `components/ui/UrgencyBadge.tsx` |
| Per-second countdown timers | Ticking-clock pressure; not "unhurried" | `components/DropCountdown.tsx` (now static) |
| Free-shipping progress meters | Gamified cart anxiety | `components/ui/FreeShippingBar.tsx` (`@deprecated`) |
| "You may also like" generic upsells | Algorithmic impulse bait | `components/CartUpsells.tsx` (`@deprecated`) |
| Green Sparkles "Save $X" celebrations | E-commerce carnival; treats a $30 set bonus like a jackpot | `pages/Cart.tsx`, `pages/Checkout.tsx` |
| "RECOMMENDED" badge on payment method | Algorithmic pressure to pick a path | `pages/Checkout.tsx` (removed) |
| Shouty ALL CAPS shipping footers | Old streetwear-volume register that says "FREE SHIPPING ON ALL ORDERS OVER $200" | `pages/ProductDetails.tsx` (now sentence-case) |

## The Five Levers (ranked by leverage-per-effort)

1. **Silence the Alarms** — strip pulsing/red/gamified urgency from badges, timers, promo bars. Files: `components/ui/UrgencyBadge.tsx`, `components/DropCountdown.tsx`. Deleted entirely: `components/PromoBar.tsx` (was a flashing "FLASH SALE / Ends Soon" bar) and `components/ui/CountdownTimer.tsx` (was a red pulsing per-second clock).
2. **Scarcity → Provenance** — restyle yellow/warning scarcity copy to neutral archival; "12 / 44 minted" stands on its own without yellow highlighting. Files: `pages/ProductDetails.tsx` (tier callout), `components/ui/UrgencyBadge.tsx`. "Claimed" is reserved for 1/1 provenance; standard catalog items that ran out read "Archived" so the word isn't diluted.
3. **De-clutter the Cart Handoff** — remove free-shipping progress bars; pare cart upsells to contextual only (the set bonus, not generic "you may also like"). Files: `components/CartDrawer.tsx`, `pages/Cart.tsx`, `pages/Checkout.tsx`. The cart should be a moment of peaceful commitment, not a final desperate sales pitch.
4. **Amplify Founder Voice** — elevate `founderNote` to sit directly under the Add to bag button on the PDP (gated on `!isUnavailable`). The founder note is the conviction-moment anti-tricky-brand voice; it should be the first thing the shopper reads after the CTA, not a buried footer element. File: `pages/ProductDetails.tsx`.
5. **Soften the Tempo** — slow `IntroScreen` exit (0.8s → 1.4s with a custom ease curve), ease cart drawer slide (~150ms → 500ms with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), drop per-second tickers. Files: `components/IntroScreen.tsx`, `components/CartDrawer.tsx`, `components/DropCountdown.tsx`.

## Kept Patterns (don't soften these)

These survived the audit because they are *not* urgency — they're status indicators, brand marks, or loading affordances. A future contributor should NOT re-treat them as urgency chrome:

- **Skeleton `animate-pulse` in `pages/Home.tsx`** (lines ~129, ~270) — loading indicators for the featured skeleton and the grid skeleton. Pulse is the appropriate register for "content is on its way." If you remove these, the loading state loses its affordance.
- **Donation impact `bg-purple-500/10 border-purple-500/20` + purple `Heart` in `pages/Checkout.tsx`** — "This order contributes one clothing item to a local shelter this month." This is a factual brand-value statement, not a celebration. The purple Heart is the brand mark for impact, not a savings badge.
- **Store Credit section header `Sparkles text-brand-accent` in `pages/Checkout.tsx`** — brand-accent is the brand color, not the urgency green/orange/red palette. The Sparkles here marks "this is a Coalition benefit," not "you saved money."
- **Numbered Edition badge `border-yellow-500/40` + `text-yellow-300` in `pages/ProductDetails.tsx`** — the last remaining yellow in the storefront, deliberately kept as a visual system for numbered pieces (paired with the tier callout). If the tier callout is ever re-introduced in yellow, this badge is the visual anchor that lets a customer distinguish a numbered piece from a standard one.
- **`On-Chain Asset` green dot + `ACCESS GRANTED` in `pages/ProductDetails.tsx`** — factual status indicators (an on-chain asset exists; the holder is verified). Green is fine for a verified state; the `animate-pulse` was removed because pulse-on-rest is the old anxiety register, but the green color stays because it means "verified."
- **SGCoinCard 24h price-change pill green/red in `components/SGCoinCard.tsx`** (lines ~52–54) — `bg-green-500/10 text-green-400 border border-green-500/20` for positive, `bg-red-500/10 text-red-400 border border-red-500/20` for negative, paired with `TrendingUp size={14}` / `TrendingDown size={14}`. Universal financial convention — green = price up, red = price down. This is semantic market-direction data, not urgency chrome. Don't soften to gray; a "up vs. down" pill rendered in a single color loses its meaning. If a future contributor flags this as celebration chrome, the convention is documented here so it survives.
- **SGCoinCard "Oracle Live" `animate-ping` in `components/SGCoinCard.tsx`** (lines ~55–61) — a fading orange `animate-ping` ring (`bg-orange-400 opacity-75`) paired with a static orange dot (`bg-orange-500`), sitting next to a label that reads "Oracle Live." This is the universal "live data" status pattern (GitHub status dot, Stripe dashboard, real-time charts) — pulsing outer ring + static inner dot + a label that names what's live. It signals "the on-chain feed is streaming right now," not "you must act now." The label carries the meaning; the pulse carries the affordance. If removed, the live-data status loses its visual reading.
- **CustomInquiryManager default-case `Sparkles` in `components/admin/CustomInquiryManager.tsx`** (line ~121) — the `default: return <Sparkles className="w-4 h-4" />;` catch-all case in the admin badge icon switch for inquiry types. The `Sparkles` here is a category glyph for the internal admin-tool list (the default inquiry type that doesn't have its own icon), not a customer-facing celebration. Admin tooling is outside the wedge scope — no customer sees this chrome. Future audit passes should NOT false-flag it; if a customer-facing Custom Inquiry form ever re-introduces Sparkles as urgency chrome, THAT is the violation, not this admin glyph.
- **Membership feature-comparison `Check text-green-500/50` / `X text-red-500/20` and `Check text-green-400` / `X text-red-400` in `pages/Membership.tsx`** (lines ~336, ~341) — feature-comparison table cells for the Std / VIP tier rows. Green `Check` = "this feature is included," red `X` = "this feature is not included." Semantically opposite, not celebratory — same convention used by every pricing table on the web (Stripe, Linear, Notion, Vercel). The semantic contrast IS the message; a feature-comparison column where "yes" and "no" render in the same color defeats the table. Don't soften to gray; soften any surrounding chrome (no celebrating the included cells, no pulsing on comparison rows) instead.

- **MobileBottomNav cart-count badge `bg-red-500 text-white` in `components/MobileBottomNav.tsx`** (lines ~30–34) — the round `bg-red-500 text-white ... {badge}` notification pill that surfaces live cart item count alongside the Cart icon in the bottom-tab nav. This is a **status indicator for the buyer's own intent** ("you have N items in your bag"), not synthetic pressure ("X people are viewing") or celebratory chrome ("You earned!"). The badge does not animate, does not fabricate viewership, does not race the shopper toward checkout. Red is the universal notification-badge color across mobile UI (Instagram unread count, Twitter mentions, every shopping app). Soften the surrounding chrome — not the badge. Don't drop this to gray; a notification pill in gray loses its status recognition, and the buyer loses the affordance that they have items waiting. If a future contributor flags this as celebration chrome, this is the documented keep: it's a status pill, not a viewership counter.
- **Ecosystem page Sparkles section heading-icons `text-orange-500` in `pages/Ecosystem.tsx`** — pairs like `<h2 ...><Sparkles className="text-orange-500" /> Redemption Loop</h2>` (and similar `<Sparkles text-orange-500 />` next to literal h2 headings, NOT next to celebration-framing copy like "Save $X!" or "You will earn!"). On the on-chain/token dashboard page the `orange-500` shade IS the page brand-identity, paired with `BurnTracker` and `SGCoinCard` to anchor the section heading in the dashboard's visual system. Same intent as the documented `Sparkles text-brand-accent` keep on Checkout's store credit section. If a future contributor flags this as celebration chrome, this is the documented keep: it's a section heading-icon at the brand-color tier, not a purchase-moment decoration.

## What Peace is NOT Here (anti-patterns to avoid)

- **Don't turn the site beige.** The brand is rooted in grief, resilience, and Baltimore streetwear. Keep the stark black backgrounds, chrome accents, and raw edge. Peace here is silent strength, not a spa.
- **Don't hide the price or buy button.** Being peaceful doesn't mean being obscure. Buttons stay loud, clear, and high-contrast — they just shouldn't be accompanied by fake ticking clocks.
- **Don't sanitize the Founder Voice.** "The Grind," "losses into movement," "stash cans," and the grief story stay. Peaceful intentionality is honest about what it took to build the brand, not pretending it was easy.
- **Don't replace founder voice with corporate wellness.** "Mindful shopping" / "self-care commerce" / "intentional living" as a marketing veneer is the opposite of this. The peace here is in the work, not in the language of the work.
- **Don't add a "calm mode" toggle.** The site is calm. A toggle would admit the loud mode exists, which means the calm mode is theater. Be calm by default.
- **Don't confuse "fewer features" with "more peace."** Removing urgency chrome is not the same as removing functionality. The cart still has line items, totals, and a checkout button. The PDP still has size selection, gallery, and reviews. Peace is in the *treatment* of the features, not the absence of them.

## Do Not Re-Introduce

These files were removed or deprecated as part of the wedge. Do not bring them back without a real product reason (and if you find one, write a new component with the same provenance-first framing rather than resurrecting the old one):

- **`components/PromoBar.tsx`** — **DELETED.** Was a flashing "FLASH SALE / Ends Soon" bar with pulsing `Zap` and `Clock` icons. The `components/AnnouncementBar.tsx` is the only announcement surface; it now renders a quiet brand line ("Coalition · Hand-built in Baltimore · No factory, no shortcuts"). If a future campaign needs a top-bar message, write a new component that matches the announcement-bar voice (no icons, no countdowns, no flash colors).
- **`components/ui/CountdownTimer.tsx`** — **DELETED.** Was a red pulsing per-second clock. Countdown surfaces in `components/DropCountdown.tsx` now render a static "To be announced" panel with a join-the-list CTA. If a real upcoming drop is wired in, the title + date render in place of the placeholder; the panel itself stays calm.
- **`components/CartUpsells.tsx`** — **DEPRECATED.** Was the generic "You may also like" + "Reach free shipping!" upsell. The contextual Above-as-Below set bonus is the only cart cross-sell that earns its place, and it lives inline in `components/CartDrawer.tsx`, `pages/Cart.tsx`, and `pages/Checkout.tsx`. If you need a real cross-sell (e.g., a build-pair suggestion that is product-aware, not algorithmic), write a new component from scratch with provenance-first framing.
- **`components/ui/FreeShippingBar.tsx`** — **DEPRECATED.** Was the "X away from free shipping!" progress meter + the celebratory "You've earned FREE shipping!" banner. Free-shipping policy is stated as a fact elsewhere (PDP footer, cart total panel, About page). If you need a shipping-status indicator, render the threshold as plain copy, not a fillable meter.
- **`utils/urgencyUtils.ts` — `generateViewCount`, `getRecentSales`, `hasActiveFlashSale`, `getTimeRemaining`, `formatTimeRemaining`** — **REMOVED.** All five manufactured FOMO. If a real viewer-count or recent-sales signal is needed, it should come from server-side analytics (e.g. last-hour product_detail views, last-24h paid orders), not client-side random math.

## Audit Trail and Consistency Rules

The wedge was applied as file-by-file rewrites against this framework. The "What Contradicts It" table above is the canonical file-target index. This section captures the *details that don't fit in a table row* — the follow-throughs and consistency rules a future contributor would miss if they only read the table:

- **PDP add-to-bag button routes through the same ternary as the status pill.** `pages/ProductDetails.tsx` add-to-bag button label is `isSold ? 'Claimed' : (isArchived || isSoldOut) ? 'Archived' : (selectedSize || ...) ? 'Add to bag…' : 'Select a size'`. This mirrors the `availabilityLabel` ternary exactly. If a contributor changes one ternary, they MUST change the other. "Claimed" is reserved for 1/1 provenance; standard catalog items that ran out read "Archived" so the word isn't diluted.
- **PDP per-size stock text uses a single neutral gray scale.** The old `text-red-500/50` (OUT), `text-yellow-500` (<5), `text-brand-accent` (normal) was softened to `text-gray-600` / `text-gray-400` / `text-gray-300`, and the "OUT" label was sentence-cased to "Out". The number itself communicates availability; the color doesn't need to. If a contributor sees `text-yellow-500` in git history, they should NOT re-introduce it.
- **Cart.tsx set-bonus block keeps a right-aligned `-$X` amount.** The body copy says "Set bonus applied / $X off the pair — the tee and shorts were built as a set", and a right-aligned `<span className="font-bold whitespace-nowrap text-gray-300">-$X</span>` surfaces the number visually. A previous round collapsed this into the body copy and lost the information; the right-aligned amount was restored deliberately. Don't re-collapse it.
- **Cart.tsx SGCoin reward line is sentence-case factual, not shouty celebration.** Was `bg-yellow-100 text-yellow-800` "You will earn X SGCoin with this order!"; now `border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] text-gray-300` "X SGCoin on this order". The reward is real value (redeemable for store credit); framing it as "you will earn!" is the gamified register the wedge retired.
- **Checkout.tsx "Paid with Store Credit" success state is neutral, not green celebration.** The success circle was `bg-green-500/20` with a green `Check`; now `bg-white/5` with a gray `Check`. The order being paid is a status, but the green circle was the old celebration framing. Don't "restore" the green for "status clarity" — the label "Paid with Store Credit" already communicates the state.
- **Home.tsx skeleton `animate-pulse` is intentionally kept** (see the "Kept Patterns" section above). The featured eyebrow `animate-pulse` was removed because it was on a rest-state element; the skeletons pulse because they're loading. Two different semantics, two different treatments.
- **Founder voice gating.** `pages/ProductDetails.tsx` `founderNote` renders only when `!isUnavailable && product.founderNote?.trim()`. For sold/archived pieces the archival "Claimed" / "Archived" note and the `archiveNote` are the right voices. The founder note is the "why this piece matters" voice, which belongs to the buy moment. If the gating is removed, the brand-accent border + bg will compete with the "Claimed - this exact piece is no longer available" archival note two paragraphs above.
- **Audit script gap discovered and addressed (the audit caught real drift, not just noise).** On the first run of `scripts/audit-urgency-chrome.ts`, four URG-SPARKLES-CELEBRATION hits surfaced: three in the `@deprecated` `components/CartUpsells.tsx` + `components/ui/FreeShippingBar.tsx` (their content is dead code the wedge already documents in "Do Not Re-Introduce"), and one legitimate kept-pattern in `pages/Ecosystem.tsx` (`<Sparkles className="text-orange-500" /> Redeem Loop</h2>` heading icon — orange is the page brand-color for the on-chain/token dashboard, same intent as Checkout's documented `Sparkles text-brand-accent`). The fix was twofold: (1) the audit walker now skips the two `@deprecated` files via a `SKIP_FILES` constant (the framework already declares them dead), and (2) Ecosystem.tsx's section heading-icons are now allowlisted in `URG-SPARKLES-CELEBRATION` and documented in Kept Patterns above. Pattern: the audit can and does surface real drift — a future contributor re-introducing urgency chrome would see a hard CI failure.
- **Mobile-nav audit found the mobile surfaces already aligned (zero code changes were needed).** The pass covered `components/MobileBottomNav.tsx` (the bottom-tab nav with cart-count notification badge — no `animate-pulse`, no viewership fabrication, no celebration framing; the badge is a buyer-intent status pill), `pages/Shop.tsx` (the `md:hidden` Filters toggle, the 1-column product-grid stack, the brand-accent `Zap`/`TrendingUp` chevron-equivalents next to the Category / Sort dropdowns — decorative, not urgency; the purple/blue VIP Membership Banner is a brand-voice pitch, not a "limited time!" deal), the `/shop` product-card stacking (uses the already-audited `components/ProductCard.tsx` — the red `Heart` favorite fill is a universal favorited-status convention), and confirmed that **there is no separate cart-sheet** — the slide-in cart IS `components/CartDrawer.tsx`, audited in an earlier pass. The mobile experience was implicitly aligned because the underlying ProductCard + CartDrawer were softened in prior passes; this audit added the MobileBottomNav badge to Kept Patterns above to pre-empt a future false flag.
