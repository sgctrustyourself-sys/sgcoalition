# Daily Outreach Playbook

**Coalition — Turn Losses Into Movement**
**Version:** 1.0 — 2026-10-24
**Owner:** Founder / Creative Director
**Goal:** 10 outbound contacts per day, split across brands/potential customers, models/sponsors, and community/random people. Track everything in `docs/marketing/outreach-tracker.csv`.

---

## Brand Voice (Non-Negotiable)

Speak as the founder. First person. Direct. Specific. No manufactured urgency.

**Follow these rules in every message:**

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **First person, founder-led** | "I built this," "I sew this," "I packed this." Never "our team." |
| **Specific, not hype** | Name the run size, the material, the city. No "limited quantities." |
| **No forbidden words** | "Sale", "Discount", "Limited Time", "Act Now", "Hurry", "Exclusive" — never. |
| **No manufactured urgency** | Never fake a countdown or low-stock panic. |
| **Short and human** | One short paragraph + one link. People read DMs, not essays. |

## The Daily 10 — Split

Every day, send exactly 10 outreach messages. Split:

| Category | Count | Who | Goal |
|----------|-------|-----|------|
| **Brands / potential customers** | 4 | Boutiques, streetwear shops, leather goods makers, denim/boot brands, complementary lifestyle brands | Wholesale, carry Coalition, cross-promo, or buy |
| **Models / sponsors** | 3 | Creators, influencers, models, athletes, artists who fit the aesthetic | Wear/post Coalition, UGC, sponsorship |
| **Community / random** | 3 | Followers, commenters, linktree clickers, email subscribers, Discord members, people who engaged | Just check it out, follow, share |

**Daily cadence target:** Send all 10 before 12:00 PM local. Log each one in the tracker the same day.

## Where to Find Targets

### Brands / Potential Customers
- Instagram Explore / Reels: search #streetwear, #leathergoods, #baltimore, #handmade, #menswear
- Local Baltimore shops and markets
- Complementary brands (denim, boots, watches, barbershops, coffee shops)
- Linktrees of creators in your niche — see who they partner with
- Small boutiques with 1k–50k followers (big enough to matter, small enough to reply)

### Models / Sponsors
- Instagram/TikTok creators who post fits, leather, streetwear, or lifestyle
- Local models, artists, athletes in Baltimore and nearby cities
- People who already tag similar brands in their posts
- Creators with 2k–100k engaged followers (micro-influencers reply more)
- Coalition taggers — anyone who already posted Coalition gear

### Community / Random
- Coalition Instagram followers and commenters
- People who clicked the linktree
- Email newsletter subscribers (from `/about` Newsletter capture)
- Discord members
- Anyone who DM'd or commented on a post in the last 30 days

## Follow-Up Cadence

| Day | Action | Template |
|-----|--------|----------|
| Day 0 | Initial outreach | Category template (A or B) |
| Day 3 | First follow-up | "Hey [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried. No pressure." |
| Day 7 | Second follow-up (value add) | Share a new piece, photo, or review. "New drop landed — thought of you." |
| Day 14 | Final check-in | "Last one from me — no hard feelings if now's not the time." |
| Day 14+ | Close / recycle | Mark status `Closed` or `Not Interested`. Can retry in 90 days. |

**Rules:**
- Never follow up more than 3 times.
- Never chase someone who said no.
- If they respond but aren't ready, note the date to circle back.

## Item / Picture / Payment Tracking

When a model/sponsor agrees to promote:

1. **Send the item.** Record the item name/SKU and send date in the tracker.
2. **Collect their pictures.** Ask for photos/video of them wearing or using the piece. Record `picture_received` and `picture_date`.
3. **Confirm posting.** Ask where/when they'll post. Record `posted` + platform.
4. **Payment.** If it's a paid collab, agree on the amount upfront. Record `payment_type` (free / paid / pending), `payment_amount`, and `payment_date`. If free, mark `free`.

The tracker is the single source of truth for who got what, who posted, and who got paid.

## Qualification Checklist (Before You Send)

Check these before messaging anyone:

- [ ] Their audience fits Coalition (streetwear / leather / craft / Baltimore / web3-adjacent)
- [ ] They post regularly (at least weekly in the last 30 days)
- [ ] Engagement looks real (comments, not just likes)
- [ ] They have a bio link / linktree / website
- [ ] They haven't been messaged in the last 90 days (check tracker)

## Daily Metrics to Track

Log these in the tracker every day:

| Metric | Target | How to measure |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Contacts sent | 10/day | Rows added today |
| Response rate | 20%+ | Replies ÷ sent |
| Items sent | 2–3/week | `item_sent` not empty |
| Pictures collected | 1–2/week | `picture_received` = yes |
| Posts / mentions | 1/week | `posted` = yes |
| Paid collabs | 1/month | `payment_type` = paid |

At the end of each week, review the tracker: who needs a follow-up, who got an item but hasn't posted, who got paid.

---

## What NOT to Do

- ❌ Don't use forbidden words ("Sale", "Discount", "Limited Time", "Act Now", "Hurry", "Exclusive")
- ❌ Don't copy-paste the same message to 10 people without swapping the name
- ❌ Don't follow up more than 3 times
- ❌ Don't message the same person twice in 90 days unless they replied
- ❌ Don't promise payment you can't pay — agree on amount first
- ❌ Don't send items without logging them in the tracker
