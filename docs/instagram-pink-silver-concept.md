# Instagram Drop Concept — PINK / SILVER

**Piece:** Women's Leopard Print Crop T-Shirt · **$45** · **Run:** 1 of 12 · **Sizes:** S–XL

## Creative direction — Y2K: Silver on rose

Glossy chrome type, bubble-pink framing, playful starbursts, dotted halftone texture, lime and icy-blue sticker accents, and the Coalition mark as a logo tile. The product photos stay accurate and central; a real workshop image adds maker-story and authenticity. The visual progression is hero → material/detail → collectibility → studio notes → shop CTA.
