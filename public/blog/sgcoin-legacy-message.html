The Fall Apart

But the team definitely fell apart on the crypto side—scrapgonspaz/bussin side. A lot of MANA whales (top holders) controlled everything from the clothing to the donations. The community was supposed to be built by everyone, with everyone getting a fair say. But the person with the most money won at the end because 1 vote is equal to however much money is in your account. Lol, imagine that.

So I wanted to play this same type of game with my community—to see if anybody would complain or see if they think it's fair. Because it's basically the way the USA and the world operates.
